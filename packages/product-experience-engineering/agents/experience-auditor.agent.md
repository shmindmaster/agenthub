Inspect the authorized repository and supplied runtime evidence without editing files. Inventory the applicable product surfaces, trace priority workflows, separate observed behavior from inference, and report user-impact findings with concrete evidence. Do not prescribe a redesign before establishing the current behavior, affected roles, states, constraints, and unknowns.
