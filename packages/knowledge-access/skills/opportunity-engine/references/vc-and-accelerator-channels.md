# VC, accelerator, and ecosystem channels

Verified live 2026-08-31. Re-verify URLs before a discovery run; these move.

## The structural point — read this before using any of it

**A VC portfolio job board is an index, not an ATS.** Its Apply link goes straight
to the company's Greenhouse/Lever/Ashby with a source tag appended (observed on
a16z: `?lever-source[]=portfoliojobs.a16z.com`). Applying through the board is the
**same application** as applying on the company site, merely attributed to the fund.
It confers no advantage.

**A talent network is categorically different.** You register one profile and a
human talent partner matches you to portfolio roles over time. No per-role
application, no repeated effort, and a person is carrying your name into companies.
That is the same shape as the expert-network insight: register once, get found.

**Therefore: prioritise talent networks over boards.** Use boards for discovery
and dedupe; use networks for leverage.

**Dedupe by destination ATS URL, never by board.** Any company with multiple
investors appears on every one of their investors' boards. Duplication is severe
and structural.

## The executive tier is relationship-mediated, not a form

**No firm publishes a joinable "CTO network."** The boards are the mid-tier. The
partner email is the executive tier.

- **Greylock** is the clearest and the single highest-ceiling, lowest-effort action
  available. `greylock.com/specialists/` splits **Core Talent** (ICs) from
  **Executive Talent** — named partners scoped to executive recruiting and boards,
  with **public partner emails as the only entry route**. There is no form. One
  well-aimed email is the whole channel.
- **Battery** (`battery.com/talent-recruiting/`) explicitly covers "individual
  contributor all the way to the C-suite." Inbound only.
- **Insight Onsite** (`insightpartners.com/onsite/`) is the only one with a real
  candidate-side door as well as an exec practice.

⚠️ `cxo.greylock.com` is an **enterprise-buyer** portal, not a placement register.
Not a job route.

## Aggregators — use these for discovery instead of going firm-by-firm

| Aggregator | URL | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **HiringCafe** | `hiring.cafe` | 3.6M jobs / 125K companies across 46 ATSs. Free. Filters on title, experience, **salary**, and funding stage — so a "$300K+ / CTO / VP Eng" sweep is a standing 20-minute query. Best effort-adjusted discovery available |
| **Getro community** | `community.getro.com/jobs` | ~12,863 jobs across the Getro network (Getro powers 850+ boards). Free, has **seniority + salary** facets. Noisy; covers only a subset |

**Consider has no candidate meta-search** — `consider.com/boards` is a 404. For
Consider-powered firms you must visit each board.

## Accelerators and programs

- **Y Combinator** — `workatastartup.com`. Browse public; applying needs a free
  account. One profile is read **by founders directly**, not routed through an ATS.
- **Techstars** — `jobs.techstars.com/jobs`, ~4,823 jobs, plus an open talent network
- **Wellfound** — `wellfound.com`, free profile, broad
- **Antler** — `careers.antler.co/jobs`, CXO-tagged and founding-engineer roles

**Founder programs, not job boards** — relevant only if the goal flips to founding:
South Park Commons Fellowship ($400K for 7%); Entrepreneur First **XF2 Experienced
Founders** (`apply.joinef.com`) requires 7+ years experience and is a genuine fit
for this profile.

**Dead — do not pursue:** Pioneer (stopped investing 2024) · AI Grant (no jobs or
talent page) · Neo (recruiting platform claimed but JS-rendered, no registration URL
confirmed).

## Ranked entry order

1. **Insight Partners Talent Network** — register once, auto-matched, no bar
2. **Greylock Executive Talent** — one email to a named partner. Highest ceiling
3. **First Round Talent** — confidential profile, leadership roles unlisted elsewhere
4. **Thrive** — register once; OpenAI/Anduril-tier portfolio
5. **HiringCafe standing query** — $300K+ / CTO / VP Eng / Head of AI, salary-filtered
6. **Menlo + Redpoint + Craft** — one form each, verified exec titles
7. **Khosla** — deep-tech and AI concentration
8. **Techstars / Antler** — near-zero effort, lower ceiling, posted comp

**Skip:** Pioneer, AI Grant, Air Street, ICONIQ, Benchmark, Founders Fund — no
channel exists.
