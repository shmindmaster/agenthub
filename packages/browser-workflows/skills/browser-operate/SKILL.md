---
name: browser-operate
description: Use when the task requires an agent to operate browser workflows across personal productivity, business portals, and portfolio applications.
---

Start with bsk --version, bsk status and bsk browsers --json. Choose the account/profile for this task. Open a dedicated background Agent Window with bsk session start --help for current flags. Keep the returned session ID on every command. Browser scopes separate tasks; profiles separate signed-in identities.

Observe the page, identify the role, origin and destination, and use named controls for navigation, filtering, form filling, uploads and downloads. Use bsk step for a single decided action, bsk plan for steps known from one observation, and observe again when the next choice depends on new data. Check expectations before a consequential click. Use bsk request-help for interactive login or human takeover.

For inbox and calendar administration, scheduling, account portals and document submissions, identify the account and intended recipients or destination first. Compare the visible signed-in account and destination against the requested identity immediately before submitting. If either differs, stop before entering or submitting data. Prepare the requested change, inspect the real compose or confirmation screen and act within the user's authorization. Verify the sent item, reopened record or downloaded file. If a response is ambiguous, inspect the business record before retrying. Stop only your own session.
