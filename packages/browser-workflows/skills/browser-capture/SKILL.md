name: browser-capture
description: Use when the task requires an agent to produce browser footage, screen evidence and source assets for walkthroughs and narrated videos.
---

Frame the workflow at the playback viewport. Inspect a screenshot after scrolling the decisive field into view; nested panels can clip their contents. Use bsk video --help to start, check, stop and save one authorized tab. Capture separate roles as separate takes. Record semantic actions separately with bsk record when a reproducible trace is requested.

For instructional footage, capture the real UI route from each role's normal home. The recording must show each menu, choice, scroll, entry and action when it happens; don't cut over a skipped interaction or substitute a deep link for a navigational step. Keep the whole application viewport visible. When a step/result needs emphasis, retain full-viewport evidence immediately before and after the highlight, with the overlay clear of relevant controls and context. Use only plausible synthetic data, clearly labeled as synthetic. Keep a capture/evidence map for the route and verify the result by reopening the same record in the UI. A fresh operator following the written steps from a separate role session is the reproducibility check; footage alone is not proof that the instructions work.

Keep an original take and verify the export for motion, cursor, readability and ending. BrowserSkill produces silent MP4. Hand the footage to the media workflow for narration, sequencing and timed captions. For a teaching film compare against the existing cursor-enhanced recorder when pointer motion matters. Use bsk emulate for mobile web framing and a real device for native app claims.

## Replacing capture after an interface change

Bind the take and step log to the exact release or build, environment, role, workspace, synthetic fixture, viewport and theme. Capture the full app viewport with its shell and original context before any editorial focus. Rehearse from the visible home on the target build before recording. After navigation, permissions, labels or decision semantics change, treat affected prior takes and stills as superseded and recapture the complete affected role journey. Keep historical audit evidence separate from current source assets. Record the visible control and a persisted result where the product exposes one.
