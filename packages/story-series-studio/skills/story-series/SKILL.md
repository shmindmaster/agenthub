# Story series (router)

AgentHub owns **methodology**. The separate series repository owns **story**.

## Load order

1. Confirm series repo / working tree (outside AgentHub) for private bible, casts, and media.
2. For real incidents → `dramatize-real-incident` (+ comedy craft in its references).
3. For tech teaching moments → also load `technical-storytelling` skills.
4. For privacy → `privacy-fictionalizer` before any external share.
5. For continuity → `series-continuity`.
6. For assembly → `episode-editor`.
7. Direction of tech scenes → `technical-story-director`.

## Truth tags (required)

Every beat must be tagged:

- `FACT` — observed / receipt-backed
- `DRAMATIZED` — real cause, heightened telling
- `COMPOSITE` — merged events; disclose in ledger

## Non-goals

- No private names, customers, or show bible content in AgentHub.
- Do not convert Product Demo Studio into a series engine.
- Remotion via `video.programmatic-composition` (tracked third-party), not vendored.
