# Dramatize real incident

Transform:

```text
REAL INCIDENT
     ↓
comic/dramatic premise
     ↓
character objective
     ↓
conflict
     ↓
escalation
     ↓
technical complication
     ↓
reversal
     ↓
payoff
```

Comedy craft lives in `references/comedy-escalation.md` — not a separate skill.
