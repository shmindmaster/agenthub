# Descript Finishing

Descript is optional editorial finishing after product truth, privacy, craft, and evidence approval. It cannot repair UI defects, invent product behavior, or replace the canonical render and review gates.

Before use, confirm the exact approved source bytes and a redaction-safe upload. Limit changes to the requested cleanup, captions, derivative cut, or review publication. Export to a new candidate, preserve the source, record the edit/export settings, and rerun technical checks plus any affected audio, caption, synchronization, or story review. A Descript edit is a new candidate; prior approval does not transfer.
