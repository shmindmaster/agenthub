# Firecrawl Knowledge Ingest

Confirm legitimate access, scope, retention, and whether a local authenticated profile may be used. Map the portal, scrape stable pages, interact only for required navigation, and crawl/batch within explicit limits. Capture authorization state, source metadata, content hashes, failed pages, and re-ingestion rules. Never persist credentials or expose protected content beyond its authorized audience.
