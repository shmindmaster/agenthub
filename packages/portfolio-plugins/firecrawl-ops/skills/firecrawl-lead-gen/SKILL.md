# Firecrawl Lead Generation

Define ICP, geography, company stage, inclusion/exclusion rules, fields, and freshness before searching. Use Agent or search plus structured scrape for discovery, then verify each record against a source URL. Return deduplicated records, qualification rationale, confidence, source date, and a rerun recipe. Do not collect restricted personal data or claim intent, contactability, or fit without evidence.
