# Firecrawl Lead Research

Research public company, product, pricing, team, customer, hiring, funding, news, and industry pages. Return company overview, recent activity, relevant people, talking points, evidence-backed pain hypotheses, outreach angle, source URLs, confidence, and rerun inputs. Do not fabricate personal details, infer sensitive traits, or treat an inferred pain point as confirmed customer evidence.
