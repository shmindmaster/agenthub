# Firecrawl Company Directories

Map the directory, identify pagination and canonical record pages, then use bounded crawl/scrape or Agent with a schema. Normalize names, domains, locations, categories, and source URLs; deduplicate and record missing fields rather than guessing. Respect terms, robots/access rules, rate limits, and review requirements.
