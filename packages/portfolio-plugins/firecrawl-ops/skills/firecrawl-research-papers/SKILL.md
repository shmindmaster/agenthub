# Firecrawl Research Papers

Start with Research Index paper search when available, expand from strong seeds to related/citing/referenced work, inspect canonical metadata, and verify claims in paper bodies. Fall back to search/scrape for whitepapers and technical reports. Return a literature map, methods/findings, limitations, evidence links, date/venue, and open questions. Preserve source identifiers and do not overstate preliminary results.
