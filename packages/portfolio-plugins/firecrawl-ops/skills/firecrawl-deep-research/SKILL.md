# Firecrawl Deep Research

Use for formal synthesis, not quick lookups or product picks. Collect from multiple angles with search, scrape, Agent, and research-index tools when available; prioritize primary sources and include contrarian evidence. Return executive summary, findings, detailed analysis, risks/open questions, sources, confidence, and rerun inputs. Scale query/source count to the requested depth and label inference separately from fact.
