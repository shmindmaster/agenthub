# Firecrawl Map

Use `firecrawl_map` or `firecrawl map` to find pages on a domain, optionally with a search term, sitemap mode, subdomain policy, and URL limit. Map first for docs, product, competitor, SEO, and knowledge-base work. Use the resulting allow-list to drive scrape or bounded crawl; do not treat a map as content evidence by itself.
