# Firecrawl SEO Audit

Map the site first, sample important pages with scrape, and use search for relevant SERP comparisons. Check titles, headings, canonical links, indexability signals, internal links, content gaps, and performance observations that the available evidence supports. Return prioritized findings with URLs, impact, confidence, and rerun inputs; do not infer rankings from a single query.
