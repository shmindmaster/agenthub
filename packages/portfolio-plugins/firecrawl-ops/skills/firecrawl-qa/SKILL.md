# Firecrawl QA

Discover pages with map, scrape stable content, and use interact for authorized forms, navigation, pagination, and JS-only flows. Use synthetic data, avoid destructive actions, and capture URL, steps, expected/actual result, severity, and evidence. Deduplicate findings and do not report speculative bugs. Return summary, tested pages/flows, critical-major-minor issues, positive observations, and rerun inputs. For product readiness, distinguish live-site findings from local code, CI, deployment, and real-customer evidence.
