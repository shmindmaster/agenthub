# Firecrawl Market Research

Use search, scrape, Agent, and browser interaction for public reports, investor relations pages, filings, earnings releases, industry sources, and charts. Cross-check important metrics, retain period/unit/source, and call out conflicting or estimated data. Return market overview, company comparisons, trends, risks, sources, confidence, and rerun inputs. This is research, not financial advice.
