# Code of Conduct

AgentHub is committed to a respectful, harassment-free community for everyone,
regardless of experience, identity, background, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be constructive, specific, and considerate.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Respect privacy and do not publish credentials, personal data, or private
  fleet configuration.
- Accept maintainer decisions about scope, safety, and unsupported host
  behavior without personal attacks.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate
misinformation, doxxing, credential disclosure, and sustained disruption are
not acceptable in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's
[security reporting channel](./SECURITY.md). Reports will be reviewed by the
maintainer, handled as confidentially as practical, and may result in a
warning, removal of content, temporary restriction, or permanent exclusion.

Project maintainers are responsible for applying this policy fairly and may
remove contributions or participation that violate it.

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
